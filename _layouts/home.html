What we work on

Research themes

From brain-inspired models down to reliable silicon — six threads that run through the lab’s work.

  • Hyperdimensional Computing

    Brain-inspired HDC models for NLP, sensing, security, and drug discovery — efficient, robust, and hardware-friendly.

  • Efficient & Approximate Computing

    Trading exactness for energy: approximate arithmetic, voltage scaling, and generalizable approximation across the stack.

  • Robustness & Reliability

    Resilient AI against hardware faults, adversarial inputs, and environmental variation — analyzed at the algorithm and hardware levels.

  • EDA / CAD

    Machine-learning-driven design automation: timing-error prediction, circuit error modeling, and smarter CAD flows.

  • Edge AI & Embedded Systems

    Scalable IoT and embedded intelligence — efficient neural networks running where the data is born.

  • Hardware Security

    Fuzz testing for firmware, ICS protocols, and AI models — finding faults before adversaries do.
